I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Mathematics](https://www.math.cuhk.edu.hk/) at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/), expected to graduate in 2027. My research lies at the intersection of **numerical analysis** and **machine learning**, with a focus on developing deep learning methods for solving partial differential equations, inverse problems, and scientific computing.

**Research Interests:**
- Numerical PDE
- Machine Learning for Scientific Computing
- Inverse Problems
- Scientific Computing

I am currently supervised by [Prof. Bangti Jin](https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~jbt) and [Prof. Zhi Zhou](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=). Previously, I received my B.Sc. from the School of Mathematics at [Jilin University](https://math.jlu.edu.cn/) in 2023.
