Room 222A, Lady Shaw Building

The Chinese University of Hong Kong

Shatin, New Territories, Hong Kong

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Mathematics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, expected to graduate in 2027. My research lies at the intersection of numerical analysis and machine learning, with a focus on developing deep learning methods for solving partial differential equations, inverse problems, and scientific computing.

Research Interests:

  • Numerical PDE
  • Machine Learning for Scientific Computing
  • Inverse Problems
  • Scientific Computing

I am currently supervised by Prof. Bangti Jin and Prof. Zhi Zhou. Previously, I received my B.Sc. from the School of Mathematics at Jilin University in 2023.
